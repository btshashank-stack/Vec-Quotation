📖 How to Use This Quotation Generator

Step 1: Click "📋 Load Sample" to auto-fill Terms & Conditions.

Step 2: Fill in the required information:

  • Quotation Details: Enter Quotation number, Date, and Optional datasheet link
  • Client Information: Company name, Location, and Contact Details
  • Products: Click "➕ Add Product" to add items with Name, Quantity, and Price
  • Signatories: Fill in your details for Signatory 1

Step 3: Review the Terms & Conditions sections (auto-loaded). You can:

  • Add more terms groups with "➕ Add Terms Group"
  • Add exclusion items for each product
  • Toggle "Supporting connectors and cables are included" checkbox

Step 4: Click "⬇ Generate PDF" (bottom right) to create your quotation.

💡 Tips:

  • Use calendar icons (📅) for date selection
  • Use dropdowns for exclusion status (Included/Not Included)
  • Floating buttons (bottom right) are always accessible
  • Status messages appear at bottom left after PDF generation
